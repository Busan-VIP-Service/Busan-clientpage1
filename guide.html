The Complete Experience Timeline

STEP 1 Discreet Arrival & Door Escort 📍

Before arrival, your concierge sends the confirmed address and meeting instructions. At the entrance, venue staff check your booking and guide your group inside.

STEP 2 Private Suite & Table Setup 🍸

Staff show you to your group's private room and explain the drinks and service included in your selected course. Ask what is included before ordering anything extra.

STEP 3 Hostess Lineup & Personal Choice ✨

Available companions are introduced according to your selected course tier. You choose whom you would like to spend the session with before the experience begins.

STEP 4 Happy Time & Departure 🚕

Enjoy happy time with the partner you chose during your booked session. When your time ends, settle the base course and any extras you agreed to, then head home. Staff can help arrange a taxi.
